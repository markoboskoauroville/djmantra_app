# Results of the night 12 -> 13.10.2026 (for Marko's morning)

Short answer: **V6** carries a new analysis for all four things Marko asked for, each measured against rekordbox
7.2.19 on his 150 test songs (the private repo markoboskoauroville/djmantra-test-songs). Phone testing of V6:
see the bottom (filled in as the night goes on). Details: `testing/sections/REKORDBOX_COMPARE.md`,
`testing/sections/learn/README.md`, `docs/COORDINATION.md` (who does what), `docs/HANDOVER*.md`.

## The references
- **rekordbox 7.2.19** (Marko's Mac): BPM, key, beat grid (XML export) and its phrase analysis (2556 phrase
  starts in 165 songs) = the reference. Files: `testing/sections/rekordbox_bpm_key.json`, `rekordbox.json`.
- **Engine DJ** (Denon): its BPM agrees with rekordbox on all 150 songs; where the app differed from rekordbox
  (21 songs) Engine DJ sided with rekordbox every time: the app was the outlier.
- **KeyFinder** (Mac run) and **Essentia** (cloud) as other key detectors. **djay Pro** and **All-In-One**:
  requested from the Mac (see bottom).

## 2. BPM: fixed for live drummers in V6
- 114 / 144 songs already had rekordbox's BPM. Most of the rest are live-played disco / funk (Brick House, Get
  Down On It, Best of My Love ...): rekordbox follows the drummer, the app forced one tempo.
- V6: when the beats do not keep one tempo (under 55 % fit one tempo and no steady stretch covers 15 % of the
  song) the app keeps a **beat map** that follows the drummer; steady songs keep their grid.
- Beats within 40 ms of rekordbox's: all songs **89.9 % -> 93.9 %**; live songs **63 % -> 93 %**
  (Get Down On It 14 -> 100 %, Brick House 18 -> 99 %).
- Still open: 4 songs at double / half tempo (e.g. Lotus Heart 90 vs 181.8).

## 3. Beat grid: two problems found, one still open
- The app's beat line is ~60 ms before rekordbox's on every song in the offline run. Essentia's tracker shows the
  same offset, so part of it may be the MP3 decoding; V6 logs the app's real grid on the phone to settle it
  (`testing/sections/app_analysis_v6.txt` from round 29).
- **Off-beat:** in ~27 % of songs the grid sits half a beat off (on the hi-hats). Not solved yet: a simple kick
  check and Essentia's tracker do not fix it. Next: learn the beat / off-beat choice from rekordbox's grid
  (as was done for sections), or All-In-One's neural beats as a guide.

## 4. Sections: new detector (v3) in V6
- rekordbox's phrases on this disco / funk / pop set are mostly 4 bars (also 2, 3, 5, 7), not a strict 8 / 16 / 32
  grid; the old detector assumed that grid.
- v3 = a small model **learned from rekordbox's phrases** (features the app computes from its own waveform),
  trained on half the songs, tested on the other half:
  - on rekordbox's own grid: F1 within a bar **0.40 -> 0.61**
  - end to end on the app-like grid: F1 within a bar **0.31 -> 0.50** (recall 0.21 -> 0.52)
- The matrix's bar.beat restarts at 1.1 at every section (since V2); with v3 that follows rekordbox-like phrases.
- Limit: the off-beat grid problem (3.) also moves sections; fixing it raises v3 further.
