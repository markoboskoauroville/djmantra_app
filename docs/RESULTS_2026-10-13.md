## 2. BPM: fixed for live drummers in V6
- 114 / 144 songs already had rekordbox's BPM. Most of the rest are live-played disco / funk (Brick House, Get
  Down On It, Best of My Love ...): rekordbox follows the drummer, the app forced one tempo.
- V6: when the beats do not keep one tempo (under 55 % fit one tempo and no steady stretch covers 15 % of the
  song) the app keeps a **beat map** that follows the drummer; steady songs keep their grid.
- Beats within 40 ms of rekordbox's: all songs **89.9 % -> 93.9 %**; live songs **63 % -> 93 %**
  (Get Down On It 14 -> 100 %, Brick House 18 -> 99 %).
- Still open: 4 songs at double / half tempo (e.g. Lotus Heart 90 vs 181.8).

## 3. Beat grid: both problems explained, the off-beat fixed in V7
- The app's beat line is ~60 ms before rekordbox's on every song in the offline run. **Not the app's fault:**
  Essentia (-57 ms) and djay Pro's neural tracker (-50 ms, 78 of 80 songs within 20-60 ms) show the same offset
  against rekordbox, so it is rekordbox's MP3 time base. Inside DJ Mantra both decks share one time base, so
  mixing is not affected. (V6 also logs the app's grid on the phone: `testing/sections/app_analysis_v6.txt`.)
- **Off-beat: fixed in V7.** In ~27 % of songs the grid sat half a beat off (on the hi-hats). djay Pro's neural
  tracker showed the beat is findable (79 of 80 right). V7: the beat tracker also measures the onsets of the kick's
  body (~130-175 Hz); when the grid moved by half a beat lines up with them better, the grid moves. On the 78
  steady songs: **on the beat 56 -> 74, off-beat 21 -> 3** (rekordbox as reference). Next: learn the beat / off-beat choice from rekordbox's grid
  (as was done for sections), or All-In-One's neural beats as a guide.

## 4. Sections: new detector (v3) in V6
- rekordbox's phrases on this disco / funk / pop set are mostly 4 bars (also 2, 3, 5, 7), not a strict 8 / 16 / 32
  grid; the old detector assumed that grid.
- v3 = a small model **learned from rekordbox's phrases** (features the app computes from its own waveform),
  trained on half the songs, tested on the other half:
  - on rekordbox's own grid: F1 within a bar **0.40 -> 0.61**
  - end to end on the app-like grid: F1 within a bar **0.31 -> 0.50** (recall 0.21 -> 0.52)
- The matrix's bar.beat restarts at 1.1 at every section (since V2); with v3 that follows rekordbox-like phrases.
- The off-beat fix (3., V7) also puts the sections on the right bars more often.
